# WhisperWatch technical summary

WhisperWatch helps subtitle editors prioritize human review for short English recordings. Whisper Large V3 generates the transcript. A frozen scikit-learn gradient-boosted tree classifier estimates risk of high transcription error. The batch review system sorts clips by a 50/50 average of the classifier and native Whisper percentile ranks.

The model was trained on 1,085,612 eligible English Common Voice 17 clips from the `train` split of Whispered, revision `25299fd560dde4271f5177118354af5a961b92fd`. The training target was WER strictly above 0.20. Training uses `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` with 200 maximum iterations, at most 15 leaves, a 100-sample minimum leaf, early stopping, and seed 42. The frozen gradient-boosted artifact includes native missing-value handling. Development comparisons included the historical logistic classifier and native Whisper confidence. The 50/50 weight was selected on the speaker-separated development tuning partition from 25/75, 50/50 and 75/25 candidates. [Whispered dataset and provenance](https://huggingface.co/datasets/burakaydinofficial/Whispered)

The ten inputs are `avg_logprob`, `min_logprob`, `mean_no_speech_prob`, `max_no_speech_prob`, `avg_compression_ratio`, `num_segments`, `duration_s`, `num_words`, `num_chars` and `words_per_second`. Averages and extrema use finite segment values. Transcript length counts use the stripped Whisper transcript. The feature extractor accepts saved segment dictionaries and live faster-whisper objects. Missing measurements are passed as `NaN`. The positive-class score is a priority signal. The single-clip UI gets contextual guidance since the historical development cutoff was never validated for live uploads.

References and WER define labels and enable evaluation. The inference feature allowlist excludes references, WER, ground-truth labels and reference-aligned words. Batch confidence is negative average log probability, with missing confidence placed first. Rank percentiles use average ties. Review lists sort descending with ascending clip ID for equal risks. Production selects `ceil(0.20 × clips)` review slots. The latest audio-aware experiment used floor rounding, which also gave exactly 128 slots for its 640 clips.

| Verified cohort | Reviewed | High error | Random caught | Native caught | Original hybrid caught |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Archived Common Voice development verification | 1,631 of 8,152 | 1,075 | — | 705 | 715 |
| Live LibriSpeech audiobook holdout | 100 of 499 | 22 | 4 | 14 | 15 |
| First live AMI holdout | 150 of 750 | 256 | 52 | 89 | 91 |
| Separate live AMI audio-feature holdout | 128 of 640 | 244 | — | 81 | 80 |

Risk-based prioritization caught more high-error clips than random on the LibriSpeech and first AMI live holdouts. The original hybrid showed small gains and a loss across live cohorts. We have no consistent evidence of a gain over native confidence. The earlier AMI-retrained hybrid caught 84 versus 89 for native confidence. The latest audio-aware hybrid caught 83 versus 81 with paired recall difference interval −3.7 to +3.2 points. Those experimental artifacts remain separate. The original Whispered test run evaluated the historical logistic baseline. The final hybrid comparison did not tune on that test data. See [the evaluation handoff](EVALUATION_HANDOFF.md) for record-level reproduction. [LibriSpeech source](https://www.openslr.org/12/) · [AMI source](https://huggingface.co/datasets/edinburghcstr/ami)

The live setup uses Systran’s Large V3 revision `edaa852ec7e145841d8ffdb056a99866b5f0a478`, faster-whisper 1.2.1, CTranslate2 4.9.0, PyAV 16.0.1, English transcription, beam 5, temperature fallback 0.0 through 1.0 in 0.2 steps, VAD off, no word timestamps, and mono 16 kHz decoding. Colab runs GPU float16 with CUDA 12 and cuDNN 9. scikit-learn 1.8.0 and joblib 1.5.3 load the saved tree artifact. On 80 diagnostic recordings with both sources available, live average log probability was lower on all 80 and only 12 of the 16 hybrid review choices overlapped. The archive lacks full decoding and preprocessing details, so the cause of the shift remains unclear. These results come from audiobooks and headset meetings. Generalization to other subtitle recordings remains uncertain. [Large V3 model card](https://huggingface.co/Systran/faster-whisper-large-v3) · [faster-whisper CUDA guidance](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper#gpu)

To run the live demo, open `notebooks/backend_colab.ipynb` in hosted Colab with a T4 GPU. The notebook installs the dependencies and checksum-verifies the classifier ZIP supplied by the team, runs `python -m src.backend_smoke`, then opens the Gradio app. The first live request can fetch about 3.1 GB of Whisper weights. See the [backend handoff](BACKEND_HANDOFF.md) for the frontend callback and dependency details.
